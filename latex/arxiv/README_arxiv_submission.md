## How to submit to arXiv

1. Create/verify your account at https://arxiv.org
2. Start a new submission, category: physics.flu-dyn (Fluid Dynamics)
3. First-time submitters need an endorsement: the submission form shows a
   6-character endorsement code and a link — send it to any researcher who has
   authored a physics.flu-dyn (or closely related) arXiv submission in the
   last five years and ask them to endorse you at the link.
4. Upload `arxiv_aero-bls_source.tar.gz` as the submission file (TeX source is
   preferred over PDF-only).
5. Metadata:
   - Title: Transition-Sensitive RANS Modelling of Boundary-Layer Separation on
     NACA 4-Digit Airfoils at Low Reynolds Number: A Validated 2D Study with
     k–ω SST and γ–Reθt Closures
   - Authors: Md Mushfiqur Rahim (ORCID 0009-0003-1021-685X)
   - Abstract: use the paper abstract
   - License: arXiv non-exclusive licence (default) or CC BY 4.0
6. Moderate wait: typically 1–5 business days (not instant). Once announced,
   the DOI-style identifier is arXiv:XXXX.XXXXX.

Note: for an instant DOI today, upload the PDF to Zenodo or OSF in parallel;
the two can cite each other.
